# Compass — Roblox Spec

**Size:** ~1.38 studs diameter × 0.34 studs tall (hand-held prop)
**Parts:** 7 mesh objects, 5 colors

## Parts & Colors

| Object | Material | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compass_Body | M_Brass | Warm Brass | #C78C0A |
| Compass_Bezel | M_Dark | Dark Brass | #473306 |
| Compass_Face | M_Face | Aged Ivory | #D9CC A6 |
| Compass_Pivot | M_Dark | Dark Brass | #473306 |
| Compass_Needle_N | M_Needle | Deep Red (north) | #B31409 |

## Studio Notes
- Import `Compass.fbx` — 7 separate mesh parts
- Set each part's `Color` property to the hex value above
- **Compass_Body** is the main brass disc case
- **Compass_Bezel** is the dark trim ring around the face
- **Compass_Face** is the aged ivory dial surface
- **Compass_Pivot** is the small center pin the needle rests on
- **Compass_Needle_N** is the red north-pointing half of the needle
- **Compass_Needle_S** is the dark south-pointing tail of the needle
- **Compass_Ring** is the small loop at the top for a chain
- Compass sits flat — place on a surface or attach to character hand
- Scale is 1 FBX unit = 1 Roblox stud — no rescaling needed on import
