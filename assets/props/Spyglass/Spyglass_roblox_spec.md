## Studio Notes
- Import `Spyglass.fbx` — 7 separate mesh parts
- Set each part's `Color` property to the hex value above
- **Spyglass_Eyepiece** is the small flared viewing cup at the left end
- **Spyglass_Inner** is the narrower inner tube pulled out from the main body
- **Spyglass_Ring_1** is the decorative band where inner tube meets main body
- **Spyglass_Body** is the main outer tube
- **Spyglass_Ring_2** is the objective trim ring at the far end — open at front, closed at back
- **Spyglass_Ring_Front** is the dark annular cap framing the lens
- **Spyglass_Lens** is the blue glass disc visible inside the objective ring
- Spyglass lies horizontally along the X axis
- Designed as a hand-held item — attach to character hand bone or use as a tool prop
- Scale is 1 FBX unit = 1 Roblox stud — no rescaling needed on import
